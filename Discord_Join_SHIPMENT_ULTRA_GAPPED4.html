01 — THE PLATFORM

More than just a chat app.

Discord is a communication platform built around servers and communities. A server can have dedicated text channels for different subjects, voice channels for live conversations, roles for permissions, and moderation tools to help organize the space.

Text channels

Keep conversations separated into topics such as general chat, announcements, rules, updates and more.
